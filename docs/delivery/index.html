Open Semantic Agent · spec 0.5

Every way in and out of the bus

Producers publish one envelope format onto the transport you already run. The OSA bus decides who gets what (topic match first, then similarity, then an AI judge only when unsure) and hands it out by pub/sub, pull, live streams or tool calls. Alerts and recommendations ride the same path, and since 0.5 the bus can also tell a person on their phone or by email.

Defined in the spec A message on its way

Requests go around the bus

OSA is for events, not for calls a user is waiting on (spec Appendix A.1). The app calls the service directly and gets its answer fast. Only after the work is done does the service publish what happened, and slower results come back to the app by push.

Each case, on its own

Every case below follows one message: where it starts, what the bus does with it, and where it ends up. The section numbers point into the spec.

New in 0.5: notifications

A subscription adds delivery.notify with channels and rules, and the bus tells the right person once per condition. Each attempt becomes a notification envelope (sent, failed, acknowledged or expired), so history(["notification.*"]) shows who was told and whether they saw it. Web Push has an exact binding; APNs, FCM and email are mapped.
